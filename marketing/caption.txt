3D LAB // 002 — AI Retail Flow Simulator ☕️

Upload a shop. Watch people use it.

For this POC, I started with one fixed-camera image of a fictional coffee shop.

Higgsfield generated an 8-second scene with five shoppers entering, browsing and queuing.

The result made one layout issue easy to discuss:
the entrance, browsing and checkout paths converged beside the central display island.

So I changed one thing:
→ moved the island to the perimeter
→ kept the checkout fixed
→ opened the central route
→ ran the same shopper scenario again

The second video is immediately easier to read.

This is an AI concept simulation — not measured footfall, a sales forecast or proof that the new layout performs better. The useful part is seeing a layout hypothesis before moving real fixtures.

The free starter includes:
• clean upload-and-review interface
• both Higgsfield videos
• before / after shop images
• exact generation prompts
• reusable workflow and checks
