I gave AI a shop and a goal:
sell more coffee. ☕

But with two rules:
→ Don’t move checkout.
→ Keep the reserved aisles at least 1.5 m wide.

3D LAB // 002 — AI Retail Store Planner

The idea: explore a merchandising hypothesis before moving the real shelves.

In the included example, coffee moves from the rear display to an entrance-side bay. You can switch between Current and AI Layout, walk through both, inspect the floor plan and export the scene.

I built the editable visual concept in Higgsfield 3D Jutsu using Auto, then packaged the idea as a separate open-source Three.js starter with measured layout checks.

The free starter includes a clearly labelled sample proposal. Connect an API account to let GPT-6 Astra interpret a custom goal; the code validates the returned layout before showing it. Astra was not used for the Higgsfield scene.

This is AI concept visualization. A display moving closer to the entrance does not prove increased sales. That would need a real-world experiment.

Small shop dataset. Three movable displays. A fixed checkout. A working 3D viewer.
